当方は、利用者を識別できる情報を一切収集しません。本アプリはアカウント登録を求めず、氏名・メールアドレス・電話番号・生年月日などの入力を求める画面がありません。当方が運営するサーバーは存在せず、本アプリはインターネットに接続して情報を送ることがありません。

本アプリには広告がなく、アプリ内課金もありません。

4. クリップボードについて

リザルト画面で「結果をコピー」を押したときだけ、スコアなどの結果を文字としてクリップボードに書き込みます。本アプリがクリップボードの内容を読み取ることはありません。

CHROMA RUSH Privacy Policy

DEMITASSE Games ("we") sets out below how information is handled in the mobile application "CHROMA RUSH" (the "App").

1. Information we collect

We do not collect any information that identifies you. The App has no account registration and never asks for your name, email address, phone number or date of birth. We operate no servers, and the App never connects to the internet to send information.

2. Information stored on your device

To restore your progress, the App stores the following on your device only. None of it is transmitted to us or to any third party.

  • Game progress (high score, weekly records, player level, coins, the skills you have obtained and their levels)
  • The rule and gimmick you have selected
  • Settings (whether effects are shortened)

This data is removed when you delete the App.

3. Ads and purchases

The App contains no ads and no in-app purchases.

4. Clipboard

Only when you tap "Copy result" on the result screen does the App write your result (such as your score) to the clipboard as text. The App never reads the contents of your clipboard.

5. Tracking

The App does not track you across apps or websites owned by other companies. It does not access advertising identifiers.

6. Children

The App is not designed or marketed for children. Because we collect no personal information, we do not collect personal information from children either.

7. Sharing with third parties

We hold no user information, so we have none to share.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as laws change or as the App gains features. We will announce material changes in the App or on this page.
